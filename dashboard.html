Corus HR – Dashboard

Centralized view of workforce insights, activities, and HR metrics.

Module: Dashboard | Audience: Admin / HR / Manager | Last Updated: Dec 2025

Overview

The Dashboard provides a real-time snapshot of key HR information including employee headcount, attendance, leave status, recruitment progress, onboarding updates, and important announcements.
