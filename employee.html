Corus HR – Employee Management

Create and manage employee master data used across all HR modules.

Module: Employee Management | Audience: Admin | Last Updated: March 2025

Overview

The Employee Management module is the foundation of Corus HR. It allows administrators to create and maintain employee profiles that are used by payroll, leave, attendance, and other HR processes.
