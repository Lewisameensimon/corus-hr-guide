Corus HR – Loan Management

Manage employee loan requests, approvals, and repayments.

Module: Loan Management | Audience: Admin / Manager / Employee | Last Updated: December 2025

Overview

The Loan Management module allows employees to apply for loans, managers to review and approve requests, and the system to track repayments through payroll deductions.
