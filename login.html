Corus HR – Login & Authentication

Secure access control for Corus HR users

Module: Login | Audience: Admin, Manager, Employee | Last Updated: Dec 2025

Overview

The Login module authenticates users and controls access to Corus HR based on assigned roles, permissions, and account status.
