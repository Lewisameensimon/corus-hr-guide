Corus HR – Policy Document Management

Create, manage, and publish HR policy documents for employees.

Module: Policy Document Management | Audience: Admin | Last Updated: March 2025

Overview

The Policy Document module allows HR administrators to upload, manage, and publish official HR policies such as leave policies, code of conduct, and compliance documents. Policies are typically assigned based on employee location.
