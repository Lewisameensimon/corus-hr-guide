Corus HR – Recruitment Process

End-to-end process for hiring employees from manpower planning to employee creation.

Module: Recruitment | Audience: Admin / HR / Hiring Manager | Last Updated: March 2025

Overview

The Recruitment module manages the complete hiring lifecycle including recruitment budgeting, resource planning, requisition requests, resume shortlisting, interview scheduling, interview feedback, candidate selection, onboarding, orientation, and final employee creation in the system.

Step-by-Step

1
Recruitment request is raised with required details such as position, department, budget, and timeline.
2
HR reviews and approves the requested recruitment details based on budget and planning.
3
Resumes are collected, screened, and shortlisted as per job description criteria.
4
Interview dates are scheduled and communicated to candidates and interview panel.
5
Interviews are conducted and feedback is recorded using predefined feedback factors.
6
Selected candidate is finalized and onboarding process is initiated.
7
Employee orientation is completed and employee record is created in the system.
