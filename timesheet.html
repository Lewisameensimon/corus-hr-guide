Corus HR – Timesheet

Capture, submit, and approve employee working hours.

Module: Timesheet | Audience: Admin / Manager / Employee | Last Updated: March 2025

Overview

The Timesheet module enables employees to record daily working hours, submit them for approval, and allows managers to review, approve, or reject submitted timesheets. Approved timesheets can be used for payroll, billing, and productivity reporting.
