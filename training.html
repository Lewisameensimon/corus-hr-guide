Corus HR – Training

Plan, assign, and track employee training programs.

Module: Training | Audience: Admin / Manager / Employee | Last Updated: Dec 2025

Overview

The Training module helps organizations manage employee learning through structured training programs, nominations, approvals, attendance tracking, and completion records.
