Corus HR – Travel

Manage employee travel requests, approvals, and expenses.

Module: Travel | Audience: Admin / Manager / Employee | Last Updated: March 2025

Overview

The Travel module enables employees to raise official travel requests, obtain approvals, record trip details, and submit travel-related expenses for reimbursement.
